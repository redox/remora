Right Option is the default rewrite and dictation key. Press it by itself for these gestures.

Default shortcuts

GestureAction
Tap right ⌥Open the writing popover in Rewrite for a selection or Draft at the caret. Click the microphone beside the Return arrow for hands-free dictation.
Double-tap right ⌥Rewrite the selection, draft at the caret, or answer a /q question in place. Opens chat outside a text field in supported contexts.
Hold right ⌥Record when dictation is enabled. Opens a closed popover in Draft or keeps the mode if it is already open. Release to review. With dictation off, hold and release to open Rewrite.
Tap right ⌥ in the popoverCycle through Draft, Rewrite, and Insert.
Return in the popoverSubmit your typed or spoken prompt in Draft, or apply your instruction in Rewrite.
⇧ Return in the popoverAdd a new line to the draft.
EscCancel an active rewrite or discard the popover draft and close it.
⇧ ⌘ GFallback rewrite shortcut. Off by default.

Reassign the keys

In Settings › Shortcuts, change or disable the rewrite key, turn off double-tap or hold, or record a fallback chord. If another app uses the same shortcut, quit it or choose different keys.

See Enable dictation for microphone and speech model setup.
