Give an edit instruction, rewrite in place, try voice dictation, and open chat. Start with an installed, signed-in copy of Remora with Accessibility enabled.

Give an instruction

  1. Choose Settings › General › Show onboarding again and continue to Try the key, or focus a supported text field in another app.
  2. Enter the example below, select it, and tap right ⌥ once to open the writing popover.
  3. With Edit selected, type Keep it to one sentence, then choose Rewrite selection or press Return.
hey sam can we move the review to thurs? need a bit more time with the draft

The result goes back into the original field. The popover stays open for another instruction. Press Esc to close it.

Rewrite in place

With the popover closed, select text and double-tap right ⌥ to rewrite it directly. To continue what you wrote, place the cursor at the end of an unfinished sentence and double-tap with no selection. Try the same gesture in the middle of a sentence to fill a gap. Use ⌘ Z in apps that support undo.

Try dictation

Enable dictation, then close the writing popover and hold right ⌥ in a text field. The popover opens in Insert and shows your transcript as you speak. Release to review it. Press Return to insert the words, or tap right ⌥ to switch to Edit first if you spoke an instruction.

Open chat

Click the chat bar at the top of your screen or choose Open chat from the Remora menu. Try “How do I stop Remora from capturing a site?” Once you have used your Mac with capture on, ask something about that activity, such as “Which document was I reading before this?” A new installation has little history to draw from.

See rewrite behavior for empty fields and /q questions or set capture exclusions.
