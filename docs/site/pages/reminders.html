Create reminders in chat. Remora posts them as chat messages and can deliver them as macOS notifications.

Set a reminder

TypeExampleSchedule
One-off“Remind me to call Sam tomorrow at 9.”One date and time, up to two years ahead.
Recurring“Remind me every Monday at 10 to send the report.”Repeats at a local wall-clock time.
Interval“Remind me every 30 minutes to stand up.”At least five minutes between firings.
Prompt“Every weekday at 16:00, summarize what I worked on today.”Runs a read-only chat task. For interval schedules, at least 60 minutes between firings.

Ask “What reminders are active?” to list them, or “Cancel my Monday report reminder” to remove one. Up to 50 reminders can be active. A reminder missed during sleep fires once after wake and then resumes its schedule. Prompt reminders depend on model availability and background budget.

Briefs and nudges

  • Morning brief at 08:00. Summarizes yesterday and suggests what to focus on today. A missed brief can arrive before noon.
  • Evening wrap at 16:00. Recaps progress and open work. A missed wrap can arrive before midnight.
  • Nudges from 09:00 to 19:00. Surface an item that may need attention. Remora sends up to three per day, at least 90 minutes apart.

These times are local. Briefs and nudges need background memory and enough eligible evidence. They use the background model budget.

Notification controls

Use Settings › Notifications to turn proactive messages on or off or send a test notification. The proactive switch controls briefs and nudges. Your reminders can still notify when it is off. macOS notification permissions control whether alerts appear outside chat.
