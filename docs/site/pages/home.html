Work in the apps you already use

Rewrite in place

Double-tap right Option to rewrite selected text. With no selection, Remora continues your sentence or fills a gap at the cursor.

The rewrite key

Insert or edit with a prompt

Tap right Option once to open the writing popover. Insert drafts new text at the cursor from your prompt. Edit applies your instruction to the selection. Tap again to switch modes.

Insert and Edit

Speak where you type

Hold right ⌥ to dictate in English or French. Review the live transcript, then insert your words or use them as an edit instruction.

Voice dictation

Ask about your work

Open the chat bar to recall a decision, find what you left open, or see where your time went.

Chat and recall

Your memory lives on your Mac.

Remora reads text through macOS Accessibility and saves it in local SQLite. Tasks that use a cloud model send relevant excerpts directly to OpenAI under your ChatGPT plan.
